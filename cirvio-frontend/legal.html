Legal & Safety Center

Clear rules for a safer student marketplace.

These pages explain how CIRVIO works, what users can list, how payments and refunds are handled, how we use data, and how students should stay safe while buying, selling or donating study material.

Terms & Conditions

CIRVIO is a student-focused marketplace for books, notes, calculators and study materials. By using CIRVIO, you agree to use the platform honestly, follow listing rules, and communicate respectfully with buyers, sellers, donors, admins and staff.

Account rules

  • Use your real name, reachable email or phone number, and accurate student or campus details where requested.
  • You are responsible for activity from your account. Keep passwords private and report suspicious access quickly.
  • One person should not create duplicate accounts to bypass moderation, rating, suspension, limits or review decisions.

Marketplace rules

  • Listings must describe the actual item condition, price, images, edition, missing pages, writing, damage and pickup or delivery expectations.
  • CIRVIO may review, approve, reject, edit visibility, pause or remove listings that look unsafe, misleading, illegal or outside the platform purpose.
  • Users may not harass, threaten, spam, impersonate, scrape data, abuse ratings, or move transactions into unsafe channels to avoid platform safeguards.

Platform role

CIRVIO helps students discover items, message admins or sellers, and organize purchases or donations. Unless clearly stated otherwise, CIRVIO is not the publisher or owner of user-listed materials and does not guarantee academic results from any item.

If a listing or message feels unsafe, pause the transaction and contact CIRVIO support before paying or meeting.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to run a safer marketplace, support user accounts, process orders, moderate listings and improve platform reliability.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone, profile photo, city, college, course and login provider.
  • Marketplace activity such as listings, saved items, messages, orders, ratings, review status, product images and transaction support records.
  • Technical details such as device/browser type, app version, approximate location from user-provided city, cookies and security logs.

How we use information

  • To create accounts, authenticate login, prevent duplicate abuse and keep your session secure.
  • To show listings, support buyer-seller-admin communication, process orders, verify listing quality and resolve disputes.
  • To send important notifications about messages, listing reviews, orders, security updates and policy changes.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with payment, hosting, authentication, analytics or support providers only when needed to operate CIRVIO. We keep data while your account is active, while required for safety, dispute handling, legal compliance or platform records, and delete or anonymize it when no longer needed.

Your choices

You may update your profile, change password, logout, request correction, or request deletion from your profile page or by contacting support. Some transaction or safety records may be retained where required.

Refund Policy

Refund handling depends on whether the order was paid through CIRVIO-supported checkout or completed directly between students.

Eligible refund cases

  • The item was not delivered or handed over after payment.
  • The item is materially different from the approved listing, such as missing books, major hidden damage or wrong edition.
  • The seller cancels before pickup or dispatch after payment is collected.
  • A duplicate payment is confirmed by the payment provider.

Not usually eligible

  • Change of mind after accepting the item.
  • Normal used-book wear already visible in photos or description.
  • Deals moved outside CIRVIO where payment evidence or delivery status cannot be verified.

Refund process

Contact support with order ID, listing title, payment proof, photos and chat details within 48 hours of delivery or expected handover. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider timelines may apply.

Cookie Policy

CIRVIO uses cookies and browser storage to keep the app functional, remember sessions, support PWA features, save local preferences and improve reliability.

Types of storage we use

  • Essential storage for login sessions, carts, saved items, notifications and app navigation.
  • Preference storage for theme, profile state, install prompts and recently used app settings.
  • Analytics or performance tools where enabled to understand crashes, traffic and feature usage.

Managing cookies

You can clear browser storage or block cookies from browser settings, but some CIRVIO features such as login, saved items, cart and PWA offline support may stop working correctly.

Seller Policy

Sellers must list only items they own or are authorized to sell, and must describe them clearly and truthfully.

Seller responsibilities

  • Upload clear photos of the actual item, not unrelated stock images.
  • Disclose writing, missing pages, water damage, torn covers, outdated editions, locked digital access codes or calculator defects.
  • Use fair pricing and avoid bait pricing, hidden fees, fake scarcity or misleading claims.
  • Respond respectfully and complete agreed handovers or cancellations promptly.

Prohibited listings

  • Stolen items, exam leaks, answer keys sold as leaks, forged notes, pirated PDFs, copied paid courses or illegal digital material.
  • Hazardous items, personal documents, medicines, weapons, adult content or anything unrelated to student learning.
  • Listings created to collect personal data, spam users, advertise unrelated businesses or bypass moderation.

Donation Policy

Donations should help students access learning material affordably. Donated items must still be usable, legal and accurately described.

Donation standards

  • Mark an item as donation only if you do not expect payment for the item itself.
  • Reasonable delivery or pickup costs may be discussed clearly before handover.
  • Do not use donation listings to lure users into paid upsells, unrelated promotions or unsafe off-platform requests.
  • CIRVIO may prioritize donation listings for students, campus drives or verified community needs.

Safety Guidelines

Student safety comes first. Use CIRVIO tools, trust ratings, listing review status and common sense before buying, selling or donating.

Before paying

  • Check item photos, edition, condition, seller profile and rating.
  • Ask for additional photos if anything is unclear.
  • Prefer CIRVIO-supported checkout or documented payment methods.
  • Do not pay large advance amounts for unverified listings.

During handover

  • Meet in a public campus/library/cafe location when possible.
  • Tell a friend where you are going for in-person handovers.
  • Inspect the item before confirming completion.
  • Stop immediately if someone pressures you, changes terms suddenly or asks for unnecessary personal details.

Report immediately

  • Fake listings, abusive messages, harassment, suspicious payment links, stolen books or exam leak claims.
  • Users asking for passwords, OTPs, ID documents or unrelated bank details.
  • Any threat, unsafe meetup request or attempt to move you into a risky channel.

FAQs

Can I use Google login and password login with the same email?

Yes. CIRVIO keeps one account per email. If you first join with Google, you can set a CIRVIO password from the setup popup or Profile settings.

Why is my listing pending?

Listings may be reviewed for image quality, item category, safety, prohibited content and basic authenticity before going live.

Can I sell notes?

You may sell your own original notes. Do not sell pirated material, copied paid courses, leaked papers or copyrighted files you are not allowed to distribute.

How do I remove saved items?

Open the Saved page and tap the heart icon on the saved listing to remove it.

What should I do if a seller does not respond?

Use Messages to follow up once. If payment is involved or the user seems suspicious, contact CIRVIO support with the listing and chat details.
