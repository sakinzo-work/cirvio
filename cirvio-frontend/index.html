Our Story

Why We Built CIRVIO

It started with a stack of first-year engineering books gathering dust after finals — books that cost a small fortune, used for one semester, then forgotten in a hostel corner. We asked a simple question: why should every student pay full price for something a senior just finished using?

CIRVIO began as a small WhatsApp group between three friends trading notes and calculators. Today it's a trusted campus marketplace where students buy, sell and donate books, notes and study material — verified seller badges and campus-based matching keep every exchange safe and simple.

Every listing here means one less book printed, one more student saving money, and one more shelf of knowledge passed on instead of thrown away.
